Common TV Mounting Challenges We Handle

Not every wall is the same, and not every TV mount is straightforward. Our team has encountered and solved a wide range of mounting challenges in homes and condos throughout Mississauga, Oakville, and Milton. Here are some of the most common issues we handle on a regular basis.

  • Irregular stud spacing or metal studs that require special anchors and toggle bolts
  • Concrete and brick walls in condos and basements that need masonry drilling and tapcon screws
  • Hiding cables inside the wall with in-wall rated cable plates and low-voltage boxes
  • High ceilings and above-standard mounting heights requiring extended reach and ladder work
  • Above-fireplace mounting with heat concerns, cable routing limitations, and mantle clearance
  • Condo building rules and restrictions that limit drilling, anchoring, or wall modifications

Our TV Mounting Process

Every TV mounting job begins with a careful assessment of your wall type, stud locations, and desired viewing position. We use professional-grade stud finders and laser levels to ensure precise bracket placement. Once we confirm the optimal location, we select the correct bracket type for your TV size and weight, drill pilot holes, and secure the mount with heavy-duty lag bolts or masonry anchors as needed. Your TV is then carefully hung, leveled, and adjusted. We route and conceal all cables for a clean, finished look, test all connections to ensure everything works, and clean up completely before we leave.

Why Hire a Professional for TV Mounting

Mounting a TV might look simple, but improper installation can lead to serious problems. A television that falls from the wall can cause thousands of dollars in damage to your floor, furniture, and the TV itself — not to mention the risk of injury to children or pets. Many DIY installations fail because the mount was attached to drywall alone without hitting studs, or because the wrong anchors were used for the wall type. Professional mounting by HandyMaster Pro eliminates these risks entirely. We carry full liability insurance on every job, use commercial-grade hardware rated for your TV's weight, and guarantee our work. If anything goes wrong, you are fully covered — something no DIY install can offer.

TV Mounting FAQ

How much does TV mounting cost in Mississauga?

TV wall mounting in Mississauga typically costs between $100 and $250, depending on the wall type, bracket style, and cable management requirements. Standard drywall mounts on studs are on the lower end, while concrete or brick installations and full cable concealment cost more. We provide a free estimate before starting any work so there are no surprises.

Can you mount a TV on a concrete or brick wall?

Yes, we mount TVs on concrete, brick, and block walls regularly — especially in condos and basements across Mississauga. These installations require a hammer drill, masonry bits, and heavy-duty tapcon or sleeve anchors. We bring all the specialized tools and hardware needed to ensure a rock-solid mount on any wall surface.

Do you hide cables inside the wall?

Yes, we offer full cable concealment using in-wall rated cable plates, low-voltage brackets, and cable raceways. For power cables, we install code-compliant recessed outlet kits behind the TV so everything is hidden and safe. The result is a clean, wire-free look with no visible cables running down your wall.
