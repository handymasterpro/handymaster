Common Kitchen Problems

Kitchens are the most-used room in any home, and everyday wear takes a toll. Many kitchens across Mississauga and surrounding areas have issues that are easy to fix but often get ignored until they become a bigger problem. HandyMaster Pro addresses the most common kitchen concerns quickly and affordably.

  • Sagging or misaligned cabinet doors that won't close properly due to worn-out hinges or shifted frames
  • Outdated hardware and handles that make the entire kitchen look dated and tired
  • Cracked, stained, or missing backsplash tiles that expose the wall to moisture and grease damage
  • Dripping or leaking faucets that waste water and cause staining around the sink basin
  • Poor under-cabinet lighting that creates dark work areas and makes food preparation difficult
  • Wasted storage space from lack of shelving, organizers, or pull-out systems inside cabinets

Our Kitchen Service Process

Every kitchen project begins with an in-home assessment. We inspect the affected areas, discuss your priorities, and provide a clear written estimate before any work starts. Our process is straightforward: we protect your countertops and floors, remove any old fixtures or hardware, complete the installation or repair with precision, and thoroughly clean up when we are done. For backsplash work, we prepare the wall surface, apply adhesive or mortar, set the tiles with consistent spacing, and grout and seal the finished surface. For cabinet adjustments, we check every hinge, drawer slide, and latch to ensure smooth, even operation across every door and drawer. We walk you through the completed work so you can see and feel the difference before we leave.

Kitchen Service FAQ

How much does cabinet repair cost in Mississauga?

Cabinet door adjustments and hinge replacements in Mississauga typically range from $75 to $150 per hour depending on the scope of work. Simple hinge adjustments on a few doors can be completed in under an hour, while a full kitchen cabinet tune-up — realigning every door, replacing worn hinges, and tightening all hardware — may take 2 to 4 hours. We always provide a free estimate before starting so you know the exact cost upfront.

Can you install a kitchen backsplash?

Yes. We install subway tile, peel-and-stick tile, mosaic sheets, and other popular backsplash materials in kitchens throughout Mississauga, Oakville, and Milton. Our installation includes surface preparation, tile setting, grouting, and sealing. A standard kitchen backsplash typically takes one full day to complete depending on the size and material chosen.

Do you replace kitchen faucets?

Absolutely. We remove your old faucet, check the supply lines and connections for any wear, and install the new faucet with a proper seal and leak-free finish. We work with all major brands and styles including single-handle, pull-down, and touchless models. Most faucet replacements in Mississauga are completed within one to two hours.
