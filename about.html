About Our Company

HandyMaster Pro is your trusted partner for home maintenance and repair across Mississauga, Oakville, Milton, and the Greater Toronto Area. With over 10 years of hands-on experience, we have built our reputation on quality workmanship, reliability, and honest pricing.

Every project, from mounting a TV to complete floor renovation, is handled with the same level of care and precision. We focus on quality work, transparent pricing, and direct communication, giving you peace of mind that your home is in steady hands.

We take pride in our "High Care Policy" — meaning we respect your space, protect your furniture and floors during work, keep the area clean, and ensure the final result is exactly what you expected.
