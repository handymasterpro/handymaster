Common Bathroom Problems

Bathrooms endure more daily moisture exposure than any other room in your home. Over time, even well-maintained bathrooms develop issues that need professional attention. HandyMaster Pro addresses these common problems for homeowners across Mississauga, Oakville, and the GTA.

  • Cracked or peeling caulk around bathtubs, showers, and sinks that allows water infiltration
  • Dripping faucets that waste water and drive up your utility bills month after month
  • Running toilets caused by worn flappers, faulty fill valves, or deteriorating seals
  • Loose or cracked tiles on floors and walls that create trip hazards and let moisture in
  • Mold and mildew buildup around the tub surround, shower door tracks, and grout lines
  • Outdated fixtures including old faucets, showerheads, towel bars, and cabinet hardware

Our Bathroom Repair Process

Every bathroom project begins with a careful assessment. We inspect the area, discuss what you need, and provide a clear written estimate before any work starts. For caulking jobs, we fully remove the old caulk, clean and dry the surface, apply painter's tape for sharp lines, and lay a smooth professional bead of high-quality silicone that will last for years. For fixture replacements, we shut off the water supply, carefully remove the old unit, check connections for corrosion or wear, install the new fixture, and test everything thoroughly for leaks. Vanity and cabinet installations include precise measurement, leveling, secure wall mounting, plumbing reconnection, and a final check of all doors, drawers, and hardware. We protect your floors and surrounding surfaces during every job and always clean up completely before we leave. You'll walk through the finished work with us so you know exactly what was done and how to maintain it.

Bathroom Service FAQ

How much does bathroom caulking cost in Mississauga?

Professional bathroom caulking in Mississauga typically costs between $100 and $250, depending on the size of the area and condition of the existing caulk. A standard bathtub re-caulk usually takes about one hour. If grout repair or mold removal is also needed, the cost may be slightly higher. We always provide a free estimate before starting so there are no surprises.

Can a handyman replace a toilet in Ontario?

Yes, a handyman can replace a toilet in Ontario when the work involves a straightforward swap of the existing unit using the same water supply and drain connection. This includes removing the old toilet, replacing the wax ring, setting the new toilet, and connecting the water line. More complex plumbing modifications — such as moving drain lines or adding new supply pipes — require a licensed plumber with proper permits.

Do you do full bathroom renovations?

HandyMaster Pro specializes in bathroom repairs, fixture replacements, and targeted upgrades rather than full gut renovations. We handle caulking, faucet and toilet swaps, vanity installations, tile touch-ups, and accessory mounting. For larger renovation projects involving structural changes, new plumbing rough-ins, or full tiling, we can recommend trusted renovation contractors in the Mississauga area. Many of our clients choose us for the finishing touches after a renovation is complete.
