Our Assembly Process

We start by inspecting all parts and hardware before assembly begins to identify any missing or damaged components early. We follow manufacturer instructions precisely, using professional tools for tight, secure connections. Every piece is checked for stability and level alignment. For wall-mounted items, we locate studs and use appropriate anchors to ensure safe, lasting installation. After assembly, we clean up all packaging materials and debris so your space is ready to use immediately.

Furniture Assembly FAQ

How much does IKEA furniture assembly cost in Mississauga?

IKEA assembly in Mississauga typically ranges from $75 to $200 per item depending on complexity. Simple bookshelves take about 30-45 minutes, while large PAX wardrobes or BESTA entertainment units may take 2-3 hours. We provide free estimates based on your specific items.

Can you assemble furniture the same day I buy it?

In many cases, yes. We offer same-day furniture assembly service in Mississauga and nearby areas when scheduling allows. Contact us early in the day for the best availability.

Do you also mount TVs and shelves on the wall?

Yes, we provide professional wall mounting for TVs, floating shelves, mirrors, and heavy artwork. We locate studs and use proper hardware to ensure everything is securely and safely mounted.
