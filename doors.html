Common Door and Window Issues We Fix

Doors and windows take daily wear and tear, and Ontario's extreme temperature swings cause frames to expand and contract. HandyMaster Pro addresses the most common problems reported by homeowners in Mississauga, Oakville, and Milton.

  • Doors that stick, drag, or won't close properly due to shifted frames
  • Worn-out hinges, handles, and locksets that need replacement
  • Drafty doors and windows causing energy loss in winter
  • Interior doors that need upgrading for a modern look
  • Smart lock installation for keyless entry and improved security
  • Sliding closet doors that have come off track or need new rollers

Our Door Installation Process

Every door installation begins with precise measurement of the existing frame to ensure a perfect fit. We remove the old door carefully, inspect the frame for damage or alignment issues, and make adjustments as needed. The new door is hung level, hardware is installed securely, and we verify smooth operation — opening, closing, and locking — before finishing. For weather sealing projects, we install new weatherstripping and threshold seals to eliminate drafts and improve energy efficiency. We clean up all debris and old materials when the job is done.

Door and Window FAQ

How much does interior door installation cost in Mississauga?

Interior door installation in Mississauga typically costs $150-$350 per door including hardware, depending on whether the existing frame needs modification. Pre-hung doors are faster to install than slab doors that need custom fitting. We provide free estimates for all door projects.

Can you install smart locks on existing doors?

Yes, we install smart locks from brands like Schlage, Yale, and August on most standard residential doors. Installation takes about 30-60 minutes per lock, and we ensure the existing deadbolt cutout is compatible or make adjustments as needed.

Do you fix sliding closet doors?

Absolutely. Sliding closet doors commonly go off track, have worn rollers, or need guide adjustments. We repair or replace the track system and rollers so your closet doors glide smoothly again.
