Flooring Challenges in Ontario Homes

Choosing and installing the right flooring in Ontario requires understanding the local climate. Basement moisture, temperature changes, and heavy foot traffic all affect which materials perform best. HandyMaster Pro helps Mississauga and GTA homeowners select and install flooring that lasts.

  • Vinyl plank (LVP) installation for basements, kitchens, and high-moisture areas
  • Laminate flooring for bedrooms, living rooms, and hallways
  • Replacing old carpet with modern hard-surface flooring
  • Subfloor leveling and preparation for smooth, gap-free installation
  • Baseboard and quarter-round installation for a finished professional look
  • Transition strips between rooms with different flooring types

Our Flooring Installation Process

We begin with accurate room measurements and a subfloor inspection. Uneven or damaged subfloors are leveled using self-leveling compound or plywood underlayment to prevent future issues. We then install proper moisture barrier and underlayment before carefully laying each plank with precise click-lock connections. Expansion gaps are maintained along all walls to accommodate seasonal movement. Finally, we install baseboards, transition strips, and shoe molding for a clean, professional finish. All old flooring and debris are removed and the space is left spotless.

Flooring FAQ

What is the best flooring for Ontario basements?

Vinyl plank flooring (LVP) is the most popular and practical choice for Ontario basements because it is waterproof, handles temperature fluctuations well, and is durable for everyday use. We help homeowners choose the right thickness and style based on their basement conditions and budget.

How long does flooring installation take?

A standard room (12x12 feet) can typically be completed in one day including subfloor prep, installation, and baseboard fitting. Larger areas or spaces requiring extensive subfloor work may take 2-3 days. We provide accurate timelines during the estimate.

Do you remove old flooring before installing new?

Yes, we remove old carpet, laminate, or damaged flooring as part of the installation process. Removal and disposal are included in our estimate so there are no surprise costs.
