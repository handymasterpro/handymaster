Common Tiling Problems We Solve

Tile issues are among the most common complaints from homeowners in the GTA. Moisture-prone areas like bathrooms, kitchens, and entryways take a beating from daily use and seasonal temperature swings. HandyMaster Pro addresses all of these problems with professional solutions that last.

  • Cracked or chipped tiles caused by settling foundations, impact damage, or poor original installation
  • Loose tiles lifting off the floor or wall due to inadequate adhesive or missing waterproofing
  • Discoloured, crumbling, or missing grout that allows moisture to seep beneath tiles
  • Mould and mildew growth in shower surrounds and bathroom floors from failed sealing
  • Uneven tile surfaces that create tripping hazards or pooling water
  • Outdated tile patterns that drag down the look and value of your home

Our Tile Installation Process

Every tiling project begins with a thorough on-site assessment. We inspect the existing surface, check for moisture issues, measure the area precisely, and discuss your preferred tile material and layout. Before any work starts, you receive a clear written estimate with no hidden fees.

Our process includes removing old tiles or preparing the substrate, applying waterproofing membranes in wet areas, setting tiles with professional-grade thinset adhesive, cutting tiles precisely to fit around fixtures and edges, grouting with colour-matched grout, and sealing the finished surface for long-term protection. We clean up completely after every job and walk you through the completed work, including care instructions to keep your new tiles looking perfect.

Tile Installation FAQ

How much does backsplash tiling cost in Mississauga?

Kitchen backsplash installation in Mississauga typically costs between $15 and $35 per square foot for labour, depending on tile size, pattern complexity, and surface preparation required. A standard kitchen backsplash of 20-30 square feet usually takes one day to complete. We provide a free on-site estimate so you know the exact cost before we begin, with no surprise charges.

Can you replace a few cracked tiles without redoing the whole floor?

Yes, in most cases we can replace individual cracked or damaged tiles without disturbing the surrounding floor. We carefully remove the broken tile and old adhesive, prepare the surface, and set a matching replacement tile with fresh grout. The key is finding a tile that matches your existing ones in size, colour, and thickness. If an exact match is not available, we can help you source the closest option or suggest creative alternatives like accent tiles.

What tile is best for Ontario bathrooms?

Porcelain tile is the top choice for Ontario bathrooms because of its low water absorption rate, excellent durability, and resistance to temperature changes. Porcelain handles the humidity and temperature swings of Canadian homes far better than natural stone or standard ceramic. For shower floors specifically, we recommend smaller mosaic tiles with more grout lines for better grip. We also advise using a waterproofing membrane beneath all bathroom tile installations to prevent moisture from reaching the subfloor.
