Home Maintenance FAQ

Can I book a handyman for a full day of maintenance?

Yes, this is one of our most popular services. Many Mississauga homeowners book a half-day or full-day visit and we work through their entire to-do list. This is the most cost-effective way to handle multiple small repairs at once.

How often should I recaulk my bathroom?

Bathroom caulking in Ontario typically needs replacement every 3-5 years, or sooner if you notice cracking, peeling, or discoloration. Proper caulking prevents water from seeping behind tiles and causing mold or structural damage.

Do you provide seasonal home maintenance?

Yes. We offer pre-winter and pre-spring maintenance visits that include checking weather stripping, inspecting caulking, cleaning gutters, testing smoke detectors, and addressing any small repairs before they become bigger problems.
