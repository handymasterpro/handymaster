Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

Do you provide free estimates?
Yes! We provide free rough estimates over the phone or via WhatsApp if you send us photos of the project. For complex on-site assessments, a small consultation fee may apply, which is then deducted from your final invoice.

It depends on the job. Small repairs are usually charged at a competitive hourly rate with a 2-hour minimum. Larger projects like flooring or painting are provided as a fixed-price (flat rate) quote.
Are you insured and licensed?

Do you work on weekends?
Yes, we understand that our clients have busy schedules. We offer weekend appointments by request to ensure your home repairs don't interfere with your work week.
How much does a handyman charge per hour in Mississauga?
Handyman rates in Mississauga typically range from CAD $75 to $150 per hour depending on the type of work, tools required, materials, and project complexity. Smaller repairs may have a minimum service charge, while larger projects are often quoted as flat-rate estimates. HandyMaster Pro provides transparent pricing and free estimates for homeowners across the GTA.
Do I need a permit for deck repair in Ontario?
Minor deck repairs in Ontario usually do not require a permit if the structure and dimensions are not being changed. However, major structural modifications, rebuilding, or expanding a deck may require permits under the Ontario Building Code. If you're unsure, HandyMaster Pro can help determine whether your project may need municipal approval in Mississauga or surrounding areas.
What's the difference between a handyman and a contractor in Ontario?
A handyman typically handles smaller repairs, installations, maintenance, and non-structural improvements, while contractors often manage larger renovations and construction projects involving permits or specialized trades. In Ontario, regulated work such as electrical, HVAC, or plumbing may require licensed professionals. HandyMaster Pro focuses on residential handyman and home repair services throughout the GTA.
Can a handyman install light fixtures in Ontario?
A handyman may install certain light fixtures or replace existing fixtures if the work falls within Ontario regulations. More complex electrical work may require a licensed electrician. HandyMaster Pro handles common residential fixture installations safely and can recommend licensed professionals when required.
How long does drywall repair take?
Small drywall repairs can often be completed in a few hours, while larger repairs involving water damage, texture matching, or multiple coats of compound may require additional drying time over one to three days. We provide realistic timelines based on the condition of the wall or ceiling.
Is IKEA furniture assembly worth hiring out?
Many homeowners in Mississauga hire professional furniture assembly services to save time, avoid frustration, and ensure proper assembly. Large wardrobes, beds, entertainment units, and office furniture can take several hours to assemble correctly. Professional assembly also reduces the risk of damaged parts or unstable furniture.
What flooring is best for Ontario basements?
Vinyl plank flooring is one of the most popular basement flooring options in Ontario because it resists moisture, handles temperature changes well, and is durable for everyday use. Laminate flooring may also work in dry finished basements. HandyMaster Pro helps homeowners choose flooring based on budget and room conditions.
How quickly can emergency repairs be completed?
Emergency repair response times depend on location, scheduling, weather conditions, and the type of repair needed. HandyMaster Pro prioritizes urgent issues such as damaged doors, broken locks, leaks, and unsafe conditions whenever possible for customers in Mississauga and nearby GTA communities.
Do you offer handyman services in Oakville and Burlington?
Yes. HandyMaster Pro provides handyman and home repair services throughout Mississauga, Oakville, Burlington, Milton, Hamilton, Guelph, and surrounding GTA areas. Service availability may vary depending on project size and scheduling.

Yes. Many homeowners hire handyman services before listing a property to improve appearance and reduce buyer concerns. Common pre-sale projects include drywall repair, interior painting, caulking, fixture replacement, door adjustments, and minor flooring repairs.
