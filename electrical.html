Common Electrical Problems We Fix

Many homes across Mississauga and Oakville were built decades ago and still have outdated wiring, loose outlets, or insufficient lighting. These issues can be inconvenient and even dangerous. HandyMaster Pro addresses the most common residential electrical concerns safely and efficiently.

  • Flickering or dimming lights caused by loose connections or aging fixtures
  • Outdated two-prong outlets that need upgrading to modern grounded receptacles
  • Broken or cracked switch plates and exposed wiring hazards
  • Ceiling fans that wobble, make noise, or no longer work properly
  • Smart home devices (Ring doorbells, Nest thermostats, smart locks) that need professional wiring
  • Smoke detector and carbon monoxide alarm installation or battery replacement

Electrical Service FAQ

How much does electrical handyman service cost in Mississauga?

Light fixture installations and outlet replacements in Mississauga typically range from $75 to $150 per hour, depending on the complexity. Simple fixture swaps may take under an hour, while ceiling fan installations usually require 1-2 hours. We provide free estimates before starting any work.

Can a handyman replace a light fixture in Ontario?

Yes, a handyman can replace existing light fixtures, outlets, and switches in Ontario when the work involves straightforward replacements within the existing wiring. More complex projects involving new circuits or panel modifications require a licensed electrician with an ESA certificate.

Do you install smart home devices?

Absolutely. We install Ring doorbells, smart locks, Nest thermostats, smart light switches, and Wi-Fi-connected devices for homeowners across Mississauga, Oakville, and the GTA. Professional installation ensures your devices are securely mounted and properly connected.
