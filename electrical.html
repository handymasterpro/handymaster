Common Fixture-Level Problems We Fix

Many homes across Mississauga and Oakville were built decades ago and still have aged fixtures, loose outlets, or insufficient lighting. HandyMaster Pro addresses fixture-level concerns safely, and refers anything requiring an ESA permit or licensed-electrician work to a qualified contractor.

  • Flickering or dimming lights caused by loose connections or aging fixtures
  • Outdated two-prong outlets that need upgrading to modern grounded receptacles
  • Broken or cracked switch plates and exposed wiring hazards
  • Ceiling fans that wobble, make noise, or no longer work properly
  • Smart home devices (Ring doorbells, Nest thermostats, smart locks) that need professional wiring
  • Smoke detector and carbon monoxide alarm installation or battery replacement

How Our Fixture Service Works

Every project starts with a thorough assessment. We inspect the existing fixture or switch, discuss your goals, and provide a clear written estimate before any work begins. Our process includes turning off the appropriate breaker, safely removing the old fixture, connecting the new fixture to the existing wiring, mounting and securing the unit, and testing it to ensure it works correctly. We always clean up after ourselves and walk you through the completed work so you understand exactly what was done.

Common Questions

How much does fixture installation cost in Mississauga?

Light fixture installations and outlet replacements in Mississauga typically range from $75 to $150 per hour, depending on the complexity. Simple fixture swaps may take under an hour, while ceiling fan installations usually require 1-2 hours. We provide free written estimates before starting any work.

Can a handyman replace a light fixture in Ontario?

Yes, a handyman can replace existing light fixtures, outlets, and switches in Ontario when the work involves straightforward replacements within the existing wiring. More complex projects involving new circuits or panel modifications require a licensed electrician with an ESA certificate.

Do you install smart home devices?

Absolutely. We install Ring doorbells, smart locks, Nest thermostats, smart light switches, and Wi-Fi-connected devices for homeowners across Mississauga, Oakville, and the GTA. Professional installation ensures your devices are securely mounted and properly connected.
