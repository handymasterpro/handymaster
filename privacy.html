Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

1. Introduction

HandyMaster Pro ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy laws, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

HandyMaster Pro is operated as a sole proprietorship in Ontario, Canada. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information when you visit our website at https://handymasterpro.ca, contact us, or request handyman and home repair services.

This policy applies to information collected through our website, contact forms, analytics tools, embedded third-party services, and communications with us by phone, email, or WhatsApp.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you contact us through our website or directly, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Project description
  • Requested service category
  • Any additional information you voluntarily provide

Usage and Device Information

Through analytics and website technologies, we may collect usage and device information that may include IP-based identifiers and browser metadata, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the website
  • Referral sources
  • General geographic region
  • Session activity and interaction data
  • Cookie identifiers

3. How We Collect Information

Contact Forms

Our website uses Formspree to process contact and estimate request forms submitted by visitors.

Cookies and Analytics Technologies

We use cookies and analytics tools, including Google Analytics 4 (GA4), to understand how visitors interact with our website.

Embedded Third-Party Content

Certain features on our website are provided by third parties, including Google Maps, Elfsight review widgets, Google Fonts, and Font Awesome. These services may automatically collect technical information through cookies or network requests.

Direct Communications

We may collect information when you contact us by phone, email, or WhatsApp.

4. Why We Collect Information

We collect personal information for purposes including:

  • Responding to inquiries and estimate requests
  • Providing handyman and home repair services
  • Communicating with customers regarding requested services
  • Improving website functionality and user experience
  • Monitoring website performance and usage trends
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations

5. How We Use Information

We use personal information only for legitimate business purposes related to operating HandyMaster Pro and delivering requested services.

Examples include:

  • Contacting you regarding your inquiry or estimate request
  • Scheduling appointments or consultations
  • Preparing service estimates
  • Improving our website and service offerings
  • Analyzing website traffic and visitor behavior
  • Maintaining records related to customer inquiries

HandyMaster Pro does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

Some third-party service providers used by our website may store or process personal information outside Canada, including in the United States.

When information is processed outside Canada, it may be subject to the laws of the foreign jurisdiction where the information is stored or processed, including lawful access requests by courts, law enforcement agencies, or regulatory authorities in those jurisdictions.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Necessary Cookies

Session cookies (Website / Formspree) — Website functionality and form security. Duration: Session.

CSRF-related cookies (Formspree) — Protection against fraudulent submissions. Duration: Session.

Analytics Cookies

_ga (Google Analytics) — Distinguishes website visitors. Duration: Up to 2 years.

_gid (Google Analytics) — Tracks visitor sessions. Duration: 24 hours.

_gat (Google Analytics) — Limits request rates. Duration: 1 minute.

Embedded Content and Third-Party Cookies

Embedded widget cookies (Elfsight) — Review widget functionality. Duration: Varies.

Map-related cookies (Google Maps) — Embedded map functionality. Duration: Varies.

Font request logs (Google Fonts / Font Awesome) — Load fonts and icons. Duration: Varies.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

  • Contact form submissions: retained for 24 months, then deleted
  • Analytics data: retained according to Google Analytics 4 default retention settings (currently 14 months)
  • Cookie data: retained according to individual cookie expiration periods
